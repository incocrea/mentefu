Why the martial language

Your mind needs training too.

Learning to understand, train and protect your mind looks a lot like training a martial art: practice, discipline, balance, knowing when not to react. We do not teach anyone to fight. We borrow the language because it describes what is needed remarkably well.

This is not about fighting your emotions. It is about learning to relate to them without letting them run your decisions on autopilot.

The MyselfU way

Not this:

  1. Attack
  2. Counter-attack
  3. Destruction

This:

  1. Observe
  2. Center
  3. Dodge
  4. Block
  5. Deflect
  6. Disarm
  7. Repair
  8. Release

The goal is not to win the fight. It is for the fight to stop controlling you.

The first art

GuiltFu: how much of what you do comes from avoiding guilt?

We start with guilt not because it causes everything, but because when it turns chronic it organizes a surprising share of everyday suffering: self-reproach, rumination, decisions made out of fear rather than conviction.

Guilt that repairs

It recognizes a mistake, points to a harm, pushes you to repair it and teaches you something for next time.

Recognize → repair → learn → move on.

Guilt that tortures

It punishes, humiliates, paralyzes, turns a mistake into an identity and keeps you stuck in the past.

Accusation → punishment → self-reproach → rumination → more guilt.

Responsibility, yes. Self-punishment, no.

Manifesto

MyselfU exists to show you another possibility.
