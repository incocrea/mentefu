I want to tell my story

The Stories room exists so that someone can read an everyday situation and think “this happens to me too”. If you have one — you said no and felt guilty, you have punished yourself for years over something you already repaired, you cannot rest without feeling you should be doing something — we would love to read it.

Write to hello@myselfu.com with the subject “Story”. We always publish anonymously and send you the text first for approval.

I want to contribute

If you are a mental health professional, an educator or a researcher in these fields and want to review content, suggest corrections or collaborate, write to the same address with the subject “Contribute”. Corrections are especially welcome: we would rather be told we got something wrong than keep getting it wrong.

Important

This mailbox is not a counseling service and we cannot answer personal questions about your situation. If you need help, please reach out to a mental health professional or your local emergency services.
