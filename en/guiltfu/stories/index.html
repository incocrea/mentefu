GuiltFuDojoLearnDetectTrainDefendStories

Real stories, or composites of real situations, always anonymous. Each one is analyzed with the same template: what happened, which emotion appeared, which thought, who the accuser is, which rule is at work, whether there is responsibility, what can be repaired and what can be let go. Have one of your own? Tell us.
