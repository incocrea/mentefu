The Dojo arranges everything else into a journey of eight levels. Each level has a central idea, readings, an exercise and a question to take with you. The belts are a metaphor for progress: there is no exam, no certificate, and your progress is stored only in your browser.

Your progress

  1. Level 1 · RecognitionBelt white
  2. Level 2 · AwarenessBelt yellow
  3. Level 3 · AnalysisBelt orange
  4. Level 4 · BoundariesBelt green
  5. Level 5 · AutonomyBelt blue
  6. Level 6 · RepairBelt purple
  7. Level 7 · ReleaseBelt brown
  8. Level 8 · MasteryBelt black
How to train

One level a week is a good pace; one a day is too fast for anything to settle. Read, do the exercise, live with the question for a few days, and mark the level when you feel you understood it — not when you finished reading it.
