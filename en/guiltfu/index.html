GuiltFu

Feeling guilty does not prove you did anything wrong. GuiltFu trains you to read that signal before obeying it, to repair when it is called for, and to let go when there is nothing left to repair.

your belt

The path arranges the whole art into eight levels. Each level has missions of a few minutes and ends with a belt exam: missions give XP, exams give belts. Tap a station on the map to enter its level.

At your own pace: you can do one level today and the next one in a month, or walk the whole path in an afternoon. The only thing each belt asks for is its exam; when to take it is up to you.

Your progress is saved to your account and follows you to any device. Belts recognize your personal progress: they are not certifications.
